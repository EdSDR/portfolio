## Overview

This is the hypergraph from Torus Portal, rebuilt for this site. In the original it maps every
agent on the Torus network and how emissions flow between them.

## The idea

Everything here is generated from a seed: about 600 nodes and 690 links. Spheres are agents,
icosahedrons are permissions, tetrahedrons are signals, and the white one in the middle is the
root allocator everything hangs from. Permissions get their own nodes because one permission can
connect a delegator to several agents at once. That's what makes it a hypergraph.

## Under the hood

Nobody placed these nodes. d3-force-3d pushes every node away from the others, pulls linked ones
together, and runs until the layout settles, then the whole graph slowly turns. Three numbers
control the shape: link distance, charge and pull toward the center. I tuned them by eye.

The glow is a Bloom pass. The nodes use unlit materials with tone mapping turned off, so their
colors stay bright enough for Bloom to spill them into the dark around them.

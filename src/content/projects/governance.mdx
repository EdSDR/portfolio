## Overview

The governance app is where the Torus DAO does its work: submitting and voting on
proposals, reviewing whitelist applications, and tracking the cadre. The landing
is anchored by a slowly lit statue of Themis — the figure of justice — set in fog
and starlight.

## The idea

Governance UIs are usually dense tables. I wanted the entry point to feel weighty
and deliberate: a single sculpted figure emerging from the dark, lit by a drifting
spotlight, before you drop into the actual tooling.

- A real 3D model (glTF) lit with soft shadows and a moving key light.
- Volumetric clouds and a starfield for depth.
- Calm, continuous motion — no spin, just a gentle sway.

## Under the hood

Built with React Three Fiber and drei (SoftShadows, Clouds, Stars). The statue is
a glTF mesh with a simple stone material; the drama is all light, fog, and
composition rather than geometry.
